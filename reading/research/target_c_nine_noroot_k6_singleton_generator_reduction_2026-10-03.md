## 1. Nine-row generator-count lift

Let B have nine no-root rows and let

    P <= Q^9

be a rank-three external coefficient-constraint space. Put

    N = null(P),

so dim N=6.

Call P two-singleton-generated when it is generated by arbitrary actual-empty
local spaces together with at most two actual singleton local lines. Pair and
complete states contribute zero coefficient constraint.

### Lemma 1

If P is two-singleton-generated, then the nine-row virtual-canonical complete
set C_v satisfies

    d_v = |C_v| - rank H(C_v) >= 1,                         (1)

and every external nullspace relation is contained in the virtual-complete
relation span:

    L(N) subset H(C_v).                                     (2)

### Proof

Let

    Q = Q^9 / P.

Since rank(P)=3, dim Q=6. Choose six coordinate classes

    e_i + P,  i in B_0,

forming a basis of Q.

For every four-subset J of B_0, puncturing the four coordinates in J preserves
rank(P)=3. Indeed, a rank loss would give a nonzero vector of P supported in J,
equivalently a dependence among four members of the chosen quotient basis.

The punctured state has five no-root coordinates. The projected coefficient
space P_J is still generated by projected empty-block constraints plus at most
two projected singleton constraints. Hence the certified five-row slack-two
theorem applies. If C_J is its virtual-complete set, then

    |C_J| - rank H(C_J) >= 1,                              (3)

and the punctured two-dimensional nullspace N_J satisfies

    L_J(N_J) subset H(C_J).                                (4)

As in the committed eight-row lift, C_J is contained in C_v. A parent
virtual-empty block stays virtual-empty after projection whenever it remains
touched; if it becomes untouched it is absent from C_J.

For alpha in N_J, restoring the four deleted coordinates creates no local
demand on blocks omitted from C_J. On a block that became untouched,
alpha vanishes on every deleted coordinate. On a block that remains touched
but is punctured-virtual-empty, its projected empty constraint lies in P_J and
therefore annihilates alpha. Thus (4) lifts to

    L(alpha) in H(C_v).                                    (5)

The six quotient-basis coordinates identify N with a six-dimensional dual
coordinate space. For |J|=4, N_J is exactly the two-dimensional coordinate
plane on the two basis coordinates outside J. Those fifteen coordinate
two-planes span N. By linearity of L, (5) for all J gives (2).

Finally C_J subset C_v and row nullity cannot decrease when complete rows are
added. Equation (3) gives (1).

## 2. Defect consequence

Let p be the selected incomplete-root cost of a compatible nine-row state, and
let q be the number of virtual-complete blocks made incomplete in the actual
root state.

A virtual-complete block cannot be actual-empty, because its empty local space
is not contained in P. Every such changed block therefore consumes at least one
selected incomplete root row, so

    q <= p.                                                 (6)

With Lemma 1, the standard virtual-canonical accounting gives

    D(B) >= 9 + p + 3 d_v - 3 q
         >= 12 - 2p.                                       (7)

Hence:

- at p=4, every two-singleton-generated state has D>=4;
- at p=5, every two-singleton-generated state has D>=2.

Both are incompatible with the live k=6 defects D=-2 and D=-1 respectively.

Therefore every survivor in either the p=4 or p=5 d_root=1 branch has a
rank-three coefficient space that is not generated by arbitrary empty-block
constraints plus at most two singleton-block constraints.

This is a generator-count statement, not a numerical low-p theorem.

## 3. Empty constraints vanish

Let U be the span of all actual-empty local coefficient spaces, and let

    L_1,...,L_s

be the local coefficient lines of the actual singleton blocks. Pair and
complete states contribute zero coefficient constraint, so

    P = U + L_1 + ... + L_s.                               (8)

Suppose dim U>0. Since dim P=3,

    dim(P/U) <= 2.

The images of the singleton lines generate P/U. Choose at most two of those
images forming a basis of P/U. Then all of P is generated by the same arbitrary
empty-block constraints together with at most two singleton blocks, contradicting
Section 2.

Thus

    U=0.                                                     (9)

Every touched actual-empty block has a nonzero local empty coefficient space.
Consequently no touched root block is actual-empty.

The singleton lines alone generate P, and at least three singleton blocks are
needed to generate its rank three.

## 4. Exact p=4 root profile

Now take

    D=-2, k=6, d_root=1, p=4.

Write s for the number of singleton incomplete blocks and t for the number of
pair incomplete blocks. Since every selected incomplete block is touched,

    s + 2t = 4.                                             (10)

Section 3 requires s>=3. Because s has the same parity as p, (10) forces

    (s,t)=(4,0).                                            (11)

Thus every survivor has exactly four touched singleton blocks, no selected
pair block, and no touched empty block. Every other touched root block is
complete.

The four singleton lines span P. Since rank(P)=3, some three of them are
nonzero and linearly independent and form a basis of P.

The preceding depth-three synchronization proves that every selected incomplete
block is touched by at least four of the nine no-root cycles. Therefore each of
these four singleton blocks has external touch support at least four.

For any singleton basis ell_1,ell_2,ell_3, the D=-2 generalized-weight floor

    (d_1,d_2,d_3)(P) >= (3,4,6)

gives

    wt(ell_i) >= 3,
    |supp(ell_i) union supp(ell_j)| >= 4,
    |supp(ell_1) union supp(ell_2) union supp(ell_3)| >= 6. (12)

These coefficient supports are contained in the corresponding block-touch
supports.

## 5. Exact p=5 root profiles

Now take

    D=-1, k=6, d_root=1, p=5.

Again s+2t=5 and Section 3 gives s>=3. Hence exactly two profiles remain:

    (s,t)=(3,1) or (5,0).                                  (13)

There is no touched empty block.

In the (3,1) profile, the three singleton lines must all be nonzero and
independent, so they form a basis of P. The pair block contributes no
coefficient constraint.

In the (5,0) profile, the five singleton lines span P and some three form a
basis.

The one-row synchronization already proves that every selected incomplete block
is touched by at least two no-root cycles.

For any singleton basis ell_1,ell_2,ell_3, the D=-1 generalized-weight floor

    (d_1,d_2,d_3)(P) >= (2,4,5)

gives

    wt(ell_i) >= 2,
    |supp(ell_i) union supp(ell_j)| >= 4,
    |supp(ell_1) union supp(ell_2) union supp(ell_3)| >= 5. (14)

## 6. Root-nullity and external-rank saturation

Let C be the full actual complete-root set and c=|C|. Since d_root=1,

    rank H(C)=c-1.                                         (15)

For the touched complete subset C_touch subset C,

    |C_touch| - rank H(C_touch) <= 1.                      (16)

Thus every candidate violating touched-complete nullity at most one can be
discarded before any coefficient-space or external-relation completion.

There is also exact external-rank saturation. In both live branches the
root-block formula gives

    delta(B)=delta(R)+k=(c-1)+6=c+5.                       (17)

Since dim null(P)=6, the six external relation directions must all survive
independently modulo H(C):

    dim((H(C)+L(N))/H(C)) = 6.                             (18)

This is an exact necessary condition, not a lower-bound relaxation.

## 7. Reduced finite target

The d_root=1 part of the nine-no-root k=6 frontier is now reduced to the
following root profiles.

For D=-2,p=4:

- exactly four touched singleton blocks;
- no pair block and no touched empty block;
- every other touched root block complete;
- the four singleton lines span rank-three P, with a singleton basis satisfying
  generalized weights (3,4,6);
- every selected singleton block is touched by at least four no-root cycles;
- touched-complete root nullity is at most one;
- all six external relation directions must survive modulo the complete-root
  span.

For D=-1,p=5, either:

- three singleton basis blocks plus one pair block; or
- five singleton blocks spanning rank-three P.

In both p=5 cases:

- no touched empty block occurs;
- every selected incomplete block has touch multiplicity at least two;
- a singleton basis satisfies generalized weights (2,4,5);
- touched-complete root nullity is at most one;
- the external relation-rank increment is exactly six.

The next finite certificate no longer needs to enumerate empty local states or
arbitrary incomplete-root profiles in either branch. For the p=4 branch it can
enumerate four singleton blocks directly, impose the depth-three touch-support
floor before coefficient construction, and then test the nullity-one and
six-external-direction conditions.
