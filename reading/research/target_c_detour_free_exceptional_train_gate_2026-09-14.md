## LEVEL I STATUS

`STILL OPEN`

The q-k amplified bridge theorem leaves three apparent first-order escape currencies:
rhombic non-Mantel slack, exceptional-face singleton trains, and metric outer detour.
The AG42 face floor and the exact outer ledger already upper-bound the detour in terms
of the loaded-hull density. Keeping the topology variable `k=d_H+s_Z` through both
calculations eliminates detour completely.

The resulting all-Type-II theorem is

$$
\boxed{
 {\sigma_R+2S_E\over D}
 \ge
 c_{DF}+{5\over2}{k\over D}+{Q-f\over D}-o(1),
 \qquad
 c_{DF}:=\sqrt{\Delta_2}-{\pi\over4}+0.00105
 =0.167964643466509\ldots .
}
\tag{DF.1}
$$

Combining this with the exceptional-singleton two-window gate gives, for every fixed
pair of projective directions `[u],[v]` and every fixed
`0<epsilon<2 arcsin(1/10)`,

$$
\boxed{
 {\sigma_R+11(Q-f)+20T_{off}(u,v;\epsilon)\over D}
 \ge
 c_{DF}+{5\over2}{k\over D}-o(1).
}
\tag{DF.2}
$$

Thus outer-walk detour is not an independent final escape. Every full-core survivor
must pay a linear amount in actual rhombic crossing deficit or exceptional-face
structure, with an additional `5k/2` penalty for diameter-topology / stress defect.
Keeping the exact degree excess `Q-f` strengthens the gate by one full copy of that
currency; the earlier `Q>=f` relaxation loses this copy unnecessarily.

## 1. Inputs and normalized variables

Let

$$
 \Delta_2={\pi\over2\sqrt3},
 \qquad
 C_*:=2\sqrt{\Delta_2}+0.00042.
\tag{DF.3}
$$

AG42 gives

$$
 {T-q/2\over D}\ge C_*-o(1).
\tag{DF.4}
$$

Pass to a subsequence on which the normalized first-order quantities converge, and write

$$
 t={q-k\over D}\to t,
 \qquad
 \kappa={k\over D}\to\kappa,
 \qquad
 p={P_F\over D}\to p,
 \qquad
 \delta={b-P_F\over D}\to\delta.
\tag{DF.5}
$$

No near-disk perimeter limit is needed here. Every force-hull side joins distinct
unit-separated sources, so `q<=P_F`; Cauchy's perimeter-diameter inequality gives
`P_F<=pi D`. Hence

$$
 t+\kappa={q\over D}\le p\le\pi.
\tag{DF.6}
$$

The exact stopped-train and outer ledgers are

$$
 T=q-k+2f,
 \qquad
 b+Q=2(q-k).
\tag{DF.7}
$$

## 2. AG42 gives the defect-sensitive outer-detour ceiling

Using `q/D=t+kappa+o(1)` in (DF.4),

$$
 C_*
 \le {t\over2}-{\kappa\over2}+2{f\over D}+o(1),
$$

hence

$$
 {f\over D}
 \ge {C_*\over2}-{t\over4}+{\kappa\over4}-o(1).
\tag{DF.8}
$$

Write `X:=Q-f>=0`. The second identity in (DF.7) gives the exact normalized relation

$$
 2t-p-\delta
 ={f\over D}+{X\over D}.
$$

Using (DF.8),

$$
 2t-p-\delta
 \ge {C_*\over2}-{t\over4}+{\kappa\over4}+{X\over D}-o(1).
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{
 \delta
 \le {9t\over4}-p-{C_*\over2}-{\kappa\over4}-{X\over D}+o(1).
}
\tag{DF.9}
$$

For `kappa=0` and `p->pi`, this specializes to the outer-detour envelope used in the
AG42/Section 171 orbit refinement. The present form does not assume that endpoint.
The retained `-kappa/4` and `-X/D` terms are needed below.

## 3. Bridge subtraction with no loss of q

Let `S` be the singleton stopped-train count and write

$$
 S=S_o+S_E,
$$

where `S_o` has no bounded exceptional-face terminal incidence and `S_E` has at least
one. Put

$$
 \rho_{rec}=2S,
 \qquad
 \sigma_R=2T_R-4\sqrt{R_4}.
$$

The exact singleton/rhombic decomposition is

$$
 \rho_{rec}+\sigma_R=2T-4\sqrt{R_4}.
\tag{DF.10}
$$

Use

$$
 2T=q+2(T-q/2),
 \qquad
 {q\over D}=t+\kappa+o(1),
 \qquad
 {R_4}\le n\le\Delta_2D^2+{\pi\over2}D+1.
$$

The last inequality is the global Oler/isodiametric bound, so
`4 sqrt(R_4)/D <= 4 sqrt(Delta_2)+o(1)`. Then (DF.4) gives the
defect-sensitive train floor

$$
\boxed{
 {\rho_{rec}+\sigma_R\over D}
 \ge t+\kappa+0.00084-o(1).
}
\tag{DF.11}
$$

The independently audited bridge subtraction gives

$$
 2S_o\le b-P_F+o(D)=\delta D+o(D).
\tag{DF.12}
$$

Subtracting only this paid singleton population from (DF.11),

$$
 {\sigma_R+2S_E\over D}
 \ge t+\kappa+0.00084-\delta-o(1).
\tag{DF.13}
$$

Insert (DF.9):

$$
 {\sigma_R+2S_E\over D}
 \ge
 p+{C_*\over2}+0.00084
 -{5t\over4}+{5\kappa\over4}+{X\over D}-o(1).
\tag{DF.14}
$$

Finally (DF.6) gives `t<=p-kappa`. Since the coefficient of `t` in (DF.14) is
negative,

$$
 {\sigma_R+2S_E\over D}
 \ge
 {C_*\over2}+0.00084-{p\over4}
 +{5\kappa\over2}+{X\over D}-o(1)
 \ge {C_*\over2}+0.00084-{\pi\over4}
 +{5\kappa\over2}+{X\over D}-o(1).
\tag{DF.15}
$$

Because `C_*/2=sqrt(Delta_2)+0.00021`, (DF.15) is exactly (DF.1).
The `+(Q-f)/D` term is genuine and comes from the exact outer ledger, not from a
new geometric estimate.

Numerically,

$$
 c_{DF}=0.1679646434665090362\ldots .
\tag{DF.16}
$$

In particular, without using the topology bonus, every survivor satisfies the clean
two-way alternative

$$
 \boxed{
 \sigma_R\ge {c_{DF}\over2}D-o(D)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 S_E\ge {c_{DF}\over4}D-o(D).
 }
\tag{DF.17}
$$

The coefficients are

$$
 {c_{DF}\over2}=0.0839823217332545\ldots,
 \qquad
 {c_{DF}\over4}=0.0419911608666273\ldots .
$$

## 4. Eliminate detour from every fixed two-window model

The exceptional-singleton two-window theorem gives the finite incidence bounds

$$
 S_E\le S_5+6(Q-f),
 \qquad
 S_5\le10T_{off}(u,v;\epsilon).
\tag{DF.18}
$$

Therefore

$$
 2S_E\le12(Q-f)+20T_{off}(u,v;\epsilon).
\tag{DF.19}
$$

Insert (DF.19) into (DF.1). Since (DF.1) already contains one positive copy of
`Q-f` on the right, cancel that copy to obtain (DF.2) with coefficient `11`, not
`12`.

A uniform unweighted consequence follows because the three coefficients in (DF.2)
sum to 32:

$$
\boxed{
 \max\left\{
 {\sigma_R\over D},
 {Q-f\over D},
 {T_{off}(u,v;\epsilon)\over D}
 \right\}
 \ge {c_{DF}\over32}-o(1)
 =0.0052488951083284\ldots-o(1).
}
\tag{DF.20}
$$

The stronger topology-sensitive right side in (DF.2) should be retained whenever
`k/D` does not vanish.

If both `Q-f=o(D)` and `T_off=o(D)` for some fixed two-window pair, then

$$
 \sigma_R\ge(c_{DF}-o(1))D.
\tag{DF.21}
$$

The exact Mantel rationalization then gives

$$
 \liminf {M_R\over D^2}
 \ge {c_{DF}\over8}
 \left(4\sqrt{\Delta_2}+{c_{DF}\over2}\right)
 =0.0817406981276745\ldots .
\tag{DF.22}
$$

## 5. Scope and remaining obstruction

This theorem uses only:

- proof-only AG42 at `eta_grid=0.00042`;
- the exact train/outer ledgers, `q<=P_F`, Cauchy `P_F<=pi D`, and the global
  Oler/isodiametric upper bound `n<=Delta_2 D^2+(pi/2)D+1`;
- the independently audited bridge-singleton payment; and
- the proof-only exceptional-singleton/two-window incidence theorem for (DF.2).

The improvement from `12(Q-f)` to `11(Q-f)` uses the exact identity `Q=f+(Q-f)`
in the outer ledger. It does not require an extra hypothesis.

No separate near-disk limits `P_F/D->pi` or `R_4/D^2->Delta_2` are used in this
composition; any such assumptions enter only through the scope of AG42 itself.
No endpoint/common-cap hypothesis is used. No new literature theorem is imported.
Contact-isolated source points remain in the source.

The result does **not** close C-II. The coarse shell endpoint `q/D->pi`, `k/D->0` is a
binding regression, so the constant `c_DF` cannot be increased from these scalar
inputs alone. A closure still needs to turn one of the three detour-free currencies
in (DF.2) into an exact unit triangle or a same-cardinality strict sub-`D` source.
The new Groemer branch supplies a separate route through anchored almost-equilateral
triples when the true force-hull residual is sublinear.

C-II and Level I remain open.
