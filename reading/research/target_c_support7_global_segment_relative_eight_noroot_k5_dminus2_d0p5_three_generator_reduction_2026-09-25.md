## 1. Setup

Let `B=R union E`, `|E|=8`, be a hypothetical state in `D=-2, k=5, d_root=0, p=5`.

Let `P <= Q^8` be its external coefficient-constraint space. Then `rank(P)=3`, `dim null(P)=5`, and the all-depth deletion theorem gives
`d_1(P)>=3, d_2(P)>=4, d_3(P)>=6`.

At each root block: empty contributes its local empty coefficient space `E_b`; singleton contributes a line or zero space `L_b <= E_b`; pair and complete states contribute no coefficient constraint. If `s` is the number of singleton incomplete blocks and `t` the number of pair incomplete blocks, then `p=s+2t`.

## 2. A survivor cannot be two-singleton-generated

Assume that `P` can be generated by arbitrary empty-block constraints plus at most two singleton-block constraints.

The committed generator-count lift then gives a virtual-canonical complete set `C_v` with `d_v=|C_v|-rank H(C_v)>=1` and full external containment `L(null(P)) subset H(C_v)`.

Let `q` be the number of virtual-complete blocks changed to incomplete states in the actual root state. A virtual-complete block cannot be actual-empty, so each such changed block consumes at least one selected incomplete root row and therefore `q<=p=5`.

The standard virtual-canonical accounting gives `D(B) >= 8+p+3d_v-3q`. Hence
`D(B) >= 8+5+3-15 = 1`, contradicting `D(B)=-2`.

Therefore:

> **Theorem 1.** Every surviving `D=-2,k=5,d_root=0,p=5` state has a rank-three coefficient space `P` that is not generated by arbitrary empty-block constraints plus at most two singleton-block constraints.

The point is generator count, not the numerical value `p=5`.

## 3. Empty constraints vanish and three singleton generators are necessary

Let the actual empty touched blocks contribute `U = span(E_b : b is actual-empty)`, and let the actual singleton blocks have local spaces `L_1,...,L_s`.

Because pair and complete states contribute no coefficient constraints,
`P = U + L_1 + ... + L_s`.

Suppose `dim U>0`. Since `dim P=3`, `dim(P/U)<=2`. The images of the singleton lines generate `P/U`, so at most two of those images form a basis of the quotient. Thus the same actual empty constraints together with at most two singleton blocks generate all of `P`, contradicting Theorem 1.

Hence `U=0`.

It follows that the singleton lines alone generate `P`. Since no two singleton blocks may generate `P`, the minimum singleton generator count is exactly three: some three actual singleton lines form a basis of `P`, and no set of at most two does.

A touched root block has nonzero local empty coefficient space. Therefore `U=0` also implies:

> **Theorem 2.** No touched root block is actual-empty.

The generalized-weight floors now apply directly to every singleton basis `ell_1,ell_2,ell_3`: `wt(ell_i)>=3`; for each independent pair, `|supp(ell_i) union supp(ell_j)|>=4`; and `|supp(ell_1) union supp(ell_2) union supp(ell_3)|>=6`.

## 4. The incomplete-root profile has only two forms

Since `p=5` and at least three singleton blocks are necessary,
`s+2t=5, s>=3`.

Thus exactly two actual incomplete-root profiles remain:

- `(s,t)=(3,1)`: three singleton blocks and one pair block;
- `(s,t)=(5,0)`: five singleton blocks.

In the `(3,1)` profile, the three singleton lines must themselves be nonzero and linearly independent, hence form a basis of `P`. In particular all three singleton blocks are touched.

In the `(5,0)` profile, the five singleton lines span `P`, their minimum generator number is three, and at least one triple of nonzero singleton lines is a basis. The other two singleton lines may be redundant or zero.

By Theorem 2, every touched root block not among these actual singleton/pair blocks is complete.

## 5. `d_root=0` gives a strict touched-root independence filter

Let `C` be the full actual complete-root set. Since `d_root=0`,
`|C|-rank H(C)=0`, so all rows in `H(C)` are independent.

Let `T` be the touched root blocks and put `C_touch = C intersect T`. Then `C_touch subset C`, hence
`|C_touch|-rank H(C_touch)=0`.

Therefore:

> **Theorem 3.** The complete-root relation rows on every touched block not selected as singleton/pair are linearly independent.

This is stronger than the nullity-at-most-one filter used in the `D=-1,d_root=1,p=3` branch.

## 6. Exact external-rank saturation

Let `c=|C|` be the total number of complete root blocks and let `delta` be the dimension of the full relation space.

Here `|A| = 8+p+3c = 13+3c`. Since `D=|A|-3delta=-2`,
`delta=c+5`.

The complete-root rows have rank exactly `c` because `d_root=0`. Also `dim null(P)=5`, so there are only five external nullspace relation directions available. Therefore `delta=c+5` is the absolute maximum, and all five external directions must survive independently modulo the complete-root span:

> **Theorem 4.** Every survivor has external relation-rank increment exactly five over `H(C)`.

This gives a completion-independent touched-state test. Let `E_rel` be the span of the five external relation rows formed from the touched state. Untouched root blocks have zero local quotient demand against the eight no-root cycles; adding untouched complete blocks only enlarges the root span. Hence
`dim((H(C)+E_rel)/H(C)) <= dim((H(C_touch)+E_rel)/H(C_touch)) <= 5`.

Since the left side must equal five, a necessary condition is
`dim((H(C_touch)+E_rel)/H(C_touch))=5`.

Thus any touched configuration with external increment at most four is impossible without enumerating optional untouched complete blocks.

## 7. Tetrahedron-boundary compression from `d_root=0`

Let `mathcal F` be the set of distinct four-vertex supports used by the eight no-root cycles, and let `m=|mathcal F|`.

For each `F in mathcal F`, let `z_F` be its signed tetrahedron-boundary dependency on the four touched triangle/root-block rows. Put
`Z(mathcal F)=span(z_F : F in mathcal F)` and `r_F=dim Z(mathcal F)`.

Let `I_touch=T\C_touch` be the touched incomplete root blocks. By Theorem 2 these are exactly the touched singleton/pair blocks.

Restrict dependency coefficients to `I_touch`:
`rho_I : Z(mathcal F) -> Q^{I_touch}`.

If `z in ker rho_I`, then `z` is a dependency supported entirely on `C_touch`. Theorem 3 says the rows of `C_touch` are independent, so `z=0`. Therefore `rho_I` is injective and
`r_F <= |I_touch|`.

The already-proved tetrahedron-boundary lemma says:

- any four distinct `z_F` are independent;
- five distinct `z_F` are dependent only when the five supports are exactly the five four-subsets of one common five-vertex set, in which case their rank is four;
- any six or more distinct supports have boundary rank at least five.

This sharpens the two root profiles.

### Profile A: three singletons plus one pair

The three singleton blocks are touched.

If the pair block is untouched, then `|I_touch|=3`, hence `r_F<=3`. Four distinct supports would already have rank four, so `m=3`. Thus the support multiplicity is forced to `(3,3,2)`.

If the pair block is touched, then `|I_touch|=4`, hence `r_F<=4`. Therefore `3<=m<=5`. If `m=5`, the five supports must be the five tetrahedra inside one common five-vertex set.

### Profile B: five singletons

Let `h` be the number of the five singleton blocks that are touched. At least three are touched because a singleton basis has three nonzero rows.

The injectivity bound is `r_F<=h<=5`. Consequently:

- if `h=3`, then `m=3`, necessarily with multiplicity `(3,3,2)`;
- if `h=4`, then `3<=m<=5`, and `m=5` forces the common-five-vertex tetrahedral configuration;
- only the `h=5` subcase can have support-boundary rank five and escape the preceding `m<=5` compression.

No claim is made here that `r_F<=5` alone classifies the `h=5` support sets.

## 8. Reduced finite target

The original arithmetic branch `D=-2,(d_root,p)=(0,5)` has therefore been reduced to two concrete root-state families.

### A. Three singleton basis blocks plus one pair block

- the three singleton coefficient rows are a basis of `P`;
- generalized weights satisfy `(d_1,d_2,d_3)>=(3,4,6)`;
- no touched empty block is allowed;
- every other touched block is complete;
- touched complete-root rows are independent;
- if the pair is untouched, the eight no-root cycles use exactly three four-vertex supports with multiplicity `(3,3,2)`;
- if the pair is touched, at most five four-vertex supports occur, with the common-five-vertex classification at `m=5`;
- the five external relation rows must have rank increment five over the touched complete-root span.

### B. Five singleton blocks

- their coefficient lines span rank-three `P` with minimum generator number three;
- the same generalized-weight, no-empty, touched-complete independence, and external-increment-five tests apply;
- with only three touched singleton blocks, support multiplicity is forced to `(3,3,2)`;
- with four touched singleton blocks, at most five four-vertex supports occur;
- the only support-level residual not covered by the rank-four compression has all five singleton blocks touched and tetrahedron-boundary rank at most five.

The most bounded next exact computation is Profile A, beginning with the pair-untouched `m=3,(3,3,2)` subcase. It has a fixed three-singleton basis, one coefficient-free pair block, the stronger generalized weights `(3,4,6)`, touched-root independence rather than nullity one, and the same completion-independent requirement of five external relation directions.

The other unresolved arithmetic branch remains `D=-1,(d_root,p)=(0,6)`.
