## 1. Changed object and intended use

The internal stopped-train endpoint graph, with **all augmented hull
pockets retained**, has an actual positive reciprocal realization. Its
angular closure matrix is a positively rescaled transpose of a bar
rigidity matrix. Consequently the graph is simple and satisfies the
stronger $`(2,3)`$ subset bound.

The implication chain is

```math
\text{positive unloaded equilibrium}
\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{integrable reciprocal face coordinates}
\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{positive straight train segments}
\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{actual reciprocal rigidity matrix}
\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{no repeated bounded endpoint pair and (2,3) sparsity}.
```

This removes same-pair reuse from a globalized bounded-face return
argument. It does not identify route lengths with independent face
currency, establish a feasible source motion, or close (S).

## 3. Integrate the reciprocal face field

Orient a contact edge $`a\to b`$. When its two augmented incident faces
are bounded, let $`l,r`$ denote its left and right faces. We claim that
there are coordinates $`q_f\in\mathbb R^2`$, one for **every** bounded
augmented face, with

```math
q_l-q_r=\alpha_{ab}J(p_b-p_a).
\tag{R.1}
```

These coordinates are unique up to a common translation.

Draw the bounded-face dual in the interior of the convex force hull.
Its edges cross exactly the nonboundary edges of $`\widehat G`$.
All such edges are actual contacts: auxiliary edges lie on the hull.
This dual is connected. Its cycle space is generated by the dual facial
cycles surrounding the interior primal vertices. There is no such dual
face surrounding a hull vertex; all loaded vertices lie on the boundary.

For completeness, if $`v,e,q`$ are the primal vertex count, augmented
edge count and hull vertex count, then the bounded dual has
$`e-v+1`$ vertices and $`e-q`$ edges. Its cycle rank is

```math
(e-q)-(e-v+1)+1=v-q,
```

the number of unloaded core vertices. These are exactly its bounded
dual facial cycles. Equivalently, integrating round a simple dual cycle
and summing the primal vertex stars on its interior gives the same
statement.

The increment around a star at an unloaded vertex $`i`$ is, with one
consistent sign,

```math
J\sum_{ij\in C}\alpha_{ij}(p_j-p_i)=0.
```

Positive-contact equilibrium therefore makes every dual cycle increment
zero. Integrate from one chosen face along a dual path. Path independence
proves (R.1). This proof uses the actual stress and the actual augmented
faces; it does not postulate a generic reciprocal embedding.

## 4. Every internal train is a positive straight segment

Let an internal stopped train $`\tau`$ have bounded endpoint nodes $`f,g`$.
At $`f`$, orient its terminal unit contact side in the counterclockwise
boundary direction and call its vector $`w`$. At $`g`$ the corresponding
vector is $`-w`$.

Moving from $`f`$ through the train to $`g`$, the first dual increment
is $`-\alpha_eJw`$. Across a strict rhombus the exit side is opposite
the entry side. Its dual increment has the **same** sign and direction.
Induct along the train. Singleton trains, including former contact
bridges separating two pockets, require no induction. Hence

```math
\boxed{q_g-q_f=-A_\tau Jw,
\qquad A_\tau=\sum_{e\in\tau}\alpha_e>0.}
\tag{R.2}
```

In particular $`q_f\ne q_g`$; no internal train is a loop. Its force-space
segment has length $`A_\tau`$. Since contact edges partition the trains,
the exact normalized virial identity gives

```math
\sum_{\text{all stopped trains }\tau}A_\tau
=\sum_{e\in C}\alpha_e=D^2.
\tag{R.3}
```

The sum on the left of (R.3) includes outside-ended trains, even though
they have no second bounded coordinate in this construction. A force-space
length is not a source route length, a source chord length or a feasible
translation increment.

## 5. Actual rigidity matrix and simplicity

The internal angular column for $`\tau=fg`$ has blocks $`Jw,-Jw`$.
By (R.2) it equals

```math
\frac1{A_\tau}
\begin{pmatrix}q_f-q_g\\q_g-q_f\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{R.4}
```

Thus the full internal angular matrix is the transpose of the ordinary
bar rigidity matrix at the **actual coordinates** $`q`$, multiplied by
the positive diagonal matrix $`\operatorname{diag}(1/A_\tau)`$.
Lemma AR.2 says its columns are independent.

If two trains join the same unordered endpoint pair, orient both from
the same node. Equation (R.2) makes their two unit vectors parallel and
their columns proportional. Independence excludes them. This eliminates
different-direction repeated endpoint pairs as well: they cannot satisfy
the positive reciprocal identity in the first place.

The earlier
[parallel-pair cut candidate](../../research/target_c_parallel_train_pair_cut_2026-09-12.md)
already excluded same-direction repeats between physical stopping faces.
The present result adds positive-force integration, all augmented pocket
endpoints and the missing different-direction case. Its simplicity
statement is not claimed as prior art for the older parallel case.

## 6. The third dependency and exact subset bound

Let $`U`$ be any bounded endpoint node set. Restrict to trains whose
two ends are in $`U`$. Besides the two constant translation row
dependencies, the row vector with face blocks $`Jq_f`$ annihilates every
column, because

```math
(Jq_f-Jq_g)\cdot Jw=(q_f-q_g)\cdot w=0.
\tag{R.5}
```

If an induced connected component has at least two nodes, it contains
an edge; its endpoint coordinates differ by (R.2). Its two translations
and reciprocal rotation are therefore linearly independent. Column
independence gives at most $`2|U_i|-3`$ edges on that component. An
isolated node contributes no edge. Consequently

```math
\boxed{e(U)\le\sum_{i:\,|U_i|\ge2}(2|U_i|-3).}
\tag{R.6}
```

In particular $`e(U)\le2|U|-3`$ whenever $`|U|\ge2`$.
No generic-position assumption, no distinctness of nonadjacent reciprocal
points and no Laman sufficiency theorem is used. This is a necessary
rank bound for the realized matrix.

## 7. Hostile checks and limits

- Four-sided pockets retain their closure rows and their own $`q_f`$.
- A physical contact bridge has two distinct augmented bounded face
  occurrences. Its singleton force segment obeys (R.2).
- Auxiliary sides carry zero force and occur only on the primal boundary;
  they cannot create an unbalanced interior dual cycle.
- The train sign is fixed by left/right face orientation, not by an
  arbitrary sign choice per edge.
- Column independence comes from mixed rigidity with all loaded hull
  velocities equal; no isolate has been deleted from a source argument.
- The additional rotation dependency needs only one nonzero segment in
  each nontrivial component. Coincident nonadjacent $`q_f`$'s cause no gap.
- (R.6) improves a finite component deficit. It does not by itself
  improve the leading coefficient $`2|U|`$, so it cannot contradict the
  scalar survivor already recorded in the live synthesis.
