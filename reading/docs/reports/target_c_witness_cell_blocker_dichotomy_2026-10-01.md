# Target C-II -- witness-cell blocker dichotomy

Date: 2026-10-01
Base main: 661cf6f947cbea513ebd395939bf3367510fba6a
Status: proof-only; C-II remains open.

## Theorem

Fix one retained shore of the current fixed-star complete O-O rectangle and order its
trains tau_1,...,tau_m on the complement-dual path. At an active adjacent interface
i, let H_i be the nonempty switching-diameter set.

Then:

- if the adjacent projective train directions are distinct, the strip has a one-bar
  switching tangent blocker;
- if the adjacent projective train directions are equal, no one-bar switching blocker
  exists, and the nonzero KKT strip reaction is generated by exactly two switching
  diameter bars with opposite transverse signs.

Thus a genuine minimal TB2 strip occurs exactly at an adjacent train-direction
plateau. This concerns a sparse tangent/reaction certificate, not the cardinality of
the complete switching set.

## Distinct adjacent directions force TB1

Current main puts the whole retained shore in one fixed diameter witness cell.
Distinct adjacent directions therefore lie in an open cell between two consecutive
global actual-diameter directions.

Use the narrow Section-90 real lift and write

    arg(u_i) = 0,
    arg(u_{i+1}) = -epsilon,

with epsilon>0. For the physical middle-layer translation, the outside-to-middle
boundary contact vectors are

    a = u_i,
    b = -u_{i+1}.

Hence the unit tangent directions that weakly open both boundary contact families form
the arc

    Omega = [pi/2-epsilon, pi/2].

The audited adjacent-strip reaction theorem, already on the ancestry of current main,
supplies a one- or two-bar tangent certificate. Suppose no one-bar certificate exists
and choose a minimal two-bar certificate with inward switching diameter vectors
n_1,n_2.

For j=1,2 define

    J_j = {w in Omega : n_j dot w < 0}.

Minimality makes J_j nonempty and proper. If J_j were empty, bar j would itself be
nonshortening throughout Omega and hence would be a one-bar blocker. If J_j were all
of Omega, then the other bar would have to be nonshortening throughout Omega and
would be a one-bar blocker. Also J_1 and J_2 are disjoint: a tangent direction on
which both bars shorten would evade the two-bar obstruction.

Since Omega has length less than pi, each nonempty proper intersection of Omega with
a strict semicircle has a zero of n_j dot w on Omega. Thus, for each j, some
psi_j in [pi/2-epsilon,pi/2] satisfies

    n_j dot w(psi_j) = 0.

Projectively, orthogonality gives

    arg([n_j]) = psi_j - pi/2 in [-epsilon,0].

Therefore each actual diameter direction [n_j] lies in the minor projective interval
between [u_i] and [u_{i+1}]. That interval lies inside the same open witness cell.
By definition the open cell contains no global actual-diameter direction. This is a
contradiction.

Hence every active adjacent interface with distinct train directions has a one-bar
switching tangent blocker.

This strengthens the older estimate that a minimal two-bar certificate has
projective bar gap at most epsilon: here each bar direction separately would have to
lie in the empty diameter-direction interval.

## Equal adjacent directions exclude TB1

Now suppose u_i=u_{i+1}=u in the coherent narrow real lift. The two physical boundary
vectors are exactly antipodal:

    a = u,
    b = -u.

The tangent set is {Ju,-Ju}. A one-bar switching blocker n would have to satisfy

    n dot Ju >= 0,
    n dot (-Ju) >= 0.

Hence n dot Ju=0, so [n]=[u].

No switching diameter has this projective direction. In an open witness cell there is
no global actual-diameter direction [u]. In a threshold witness cell, current main's
local witness-cell theorem identifies the unique parallel diameter edge as a cut edge
for every retained train. It therefore has full support on the retained shore and is
not switching. Parallel-diameter uniqueness excludes any second diameter edge in the
same projective direction.

Thus a one-bar blocker is impossible. The audited exact-antipodal strip theorem gives
a two-bar tangent obstruction with opposite strict transverse signs.

The exact KKT statement is also two-bar. If A,B>0 are the two boundary contact masses,
reaction nondegeneracy gives

    G = A u - B u = (A-B)u != 0.

A one-bar positive representation of G would require a switching diameter parallel
to u, already excluded. Conic Caratheodory gives at most two bars, so exactly two are
needed. Because their positive combination is parallel to u while neither bar is
parallel to u, their transverse components have opposite strict signs.

## Linear consequence on the current complete grid

Current main gives

    m >= (c_* - o(1)) D

on each retained shore and the rank-switching theorem gives

    s >= (c_*/2 - o(1)) D

active adjacent interfaces. Partition these interfaces into U, where adjacent train
directions differ, and E, where they agree. The theorem identifies U with TB1 and E
with genuine exact-antipodal TB2. Therefore

    max(|U|,|E|) >= (c_*/4 - o(1)) D.

In a threshold witness cell every active interface belongs to E.

## Joint bounded and stress-light refinement

Normalize the positive diameter KKT stress by sum_h beta_h=1 and put

    mu_i = sum_{h in H_i} beta_h.

Current main proves t_A(h)+t_B(h)<=2 for every actual diameter edge. Weighting by
beta_h gives the exact joint stress-switch budget

    sum_i mu_i^A + sum_j mu_j^B <= 2.

Current main also gives

    sum_i N_i^A + sum_j N_j^B <= (2 pi + o(1)) D

and

    s_A+s_B >= (c_* - o(1)) D.

Across both shores, one of the two blocker classes therefore has at least

    (c_*/2 - o(1)) D

interfaces. Set

    H_* = ceil(16 pi/c_*),
    L_* = 16/c_*.

The joint count budget shows that fewer than (c_*/8+o(1))D interfaces have
N_i>H_*. The joint stress budget shows that at most (c_*/8)D interfaces have
mu_i>L_*/D. Hence the dominant blocker class contains at least

    (c_*/4 - o(1)) D

interfaces satisfying both

    1 <= N_i <= H_*,
    mu_i <= L_*/D.

Build a conflict graph on those interfaces. Join two interfaces if they share a
switching diameter edge, or if they are adjacent interfaces on the same shore and
therefore share a train. Each selected interface has at most two same-shore train
conflicts. Each of its at most H_* switching diameter edges can occur at at most one
other interface because t_A(h)+t_B(h)<=2. Thus the conflict graph has maximum degree
at most H_*+2.

A greedy independent set has size at least

    (c_*/(4(H_*+3)) - o(1)) D.

Its adjacent train pairs are pairwise disjoint and its complete switching-diameter
packets are pairwise edge-disjoint. For every retained interface,

    0 < |G_i| <= D mu_i <= L_*,

where the strict inequality is reaction nondegeneracy.

Thus the fixed-star branch contains linearly many pairwise-disjoint bounded,
stress-light cusps of one common exact type: phase-changing TB1 cusps, or
exact-direction TB2 cusps with nonzero axial two-bar reactions.

## Remaining target

The generic non-antipodal TB2 branch is eliminated from the fixed-star witness-cell
geometry. The two remaining fixed-star mechanisms are:

1. a linear family of fresh TB1 switching bars, where the next target is a global
   convex-thrackle/order obstruction against the common transverse train shore; or
2. a linear family of exact-direction plateaus with fresh two-bar axial reactions,
   where the next target is an ordered-chord or reaction-variation obstruction.

The split-star O-O branch and the inactive-interface branch remain separate.
