## Preferred locked-branch endpoint: full unit tube

`T_Sigma={y in Y_in:dist(y,|Sigma|)<1}`,
`T_Z=T_Sigma cap Z`,

and after Section-174 sectorization put

`Xi_Sigma=|O_iso|+sum_i max(0,|O_i|-|C_i|)`.

With

`j_*=N-|Y_0|-m^*`,

`h(G)=|O|-rank S >= Xi_Sigma-|T_Z|`,

`boxed{Xi_Sigma >= j_*+|T_Z|}`.

`|O_iso| + sum_i max(0,|O_i|-|C_i|) >= j_* + |T_Z|`.

`|T_Sigma|<=9|E(Sigma)|`.

## Alternative locked-branch endpoint: edge-lens coarse Hall

For each unit contact edge `pq`, define

`L(pq)=N_Lambda(p) cap N_Lambda(q)`

and let `L_Sigma` be the union of these right-shore lenses. Section 177 proves:

- every response edge crossing `pq` lands in the strict neighborhood of both `p` and
  `q`;
- every such lens contains at most two triangular-lattice sites;
- hence `|L_Sigma|<=2|E(Sigma)|`;

  `zeta>=delta_Sigma^lens-|L_Sigma|-beta_Sigma`, where

Therefore the exact lens-route closure is

`boxed{delta_Sigma^lens >= s+|L_Sigma|+beta_Sigma}`,

`|L_Sigma|<=2v-2`,
