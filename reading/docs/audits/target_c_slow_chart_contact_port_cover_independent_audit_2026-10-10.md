## Exact statement

Let `A_j` be an unbounded sequence of canonical contact-minimal, then diameter-pair-minimal, post-ZF triangle-free C-II global minimizers, with `C=C_*`, `H=H_*`, and diameters `D_j -> infinity`. Let `B_j` be the full set of actual diameter endpoints and `q_j=|B_j|`. Every source label, including every contact-isolated label, remains in `A_j`.

For each `j`, supply at most a fixed `J` charts

```text
phi_{j,s}(lambda) = lambda + g_{j,s}(lambda), lambda in X_{j,s} subset Lambda_{j,s},
```

where each lattice is generated by unit vectors at angle in `[pi/3,2pi/3]`, the domains are arbitrary subsets, and for a fixed `K`

```text
|g_{j,s}(lambda)-g_{j,s}(mu)| <= (K/D_j)|lambda-mu|.
```

A loaded endpoint is covered if either:

1. it has exactly one actual unit neighbor, that contact has positive coefficient in some normalized full-source KKT stress, and the neighbor is an image of one supplied chart; or
2. it has two distinct actual unit neighbors that are images of the same supplied chart.

Then

```text
|B_{j,covered}| = O_{J,K}(D_j^(4/5)),
q_j - |B_{j,covered}| >= D_j - O_{J,K}(D_j^(4/5)).
```

No chart rectangle occupancy is required. The two classes use actual source contacts; the first retains the positive-stress condition, while the second requires no stress condition.

## Independent reconstruction

For each covered leaf endpoint `p`, let `a` be its sole actual unit neighbor. Audit.247 independently proves the proposed theorem `C-POSITIVE-LEAF-ANCHOR-INNER-BALL-BOUNDARY` and gives

```text
dist(a, partial K_D) <= C D^(-2/3),
K_D = intersection_{x in A} closed_ball(x, D-1).
```

This is a full-source statement: isolates are included among the disk centers. The body `K_D` fits the hypotheses of `C-SLOW-CHART-NEAR-BOUNDARY-COUNT`. If `o` is an actual diameter-pair midpoint and `c_*=1-sqrt(3)/2`, the two diameter disks give `closed_ball(o,c_*D-1) subset K_D`. For any fixed `x_0 in A`, `K_D subset closed_ball(x_0,D-1)`. Thus its inradius is bounded below by a positive multiple of `D`, its outer radius is `O(D)`, and it is an intersection of disks of radii at most `D`.

The distinct anchors `a` are unit-separated. Each selected anchor is exactly an image in one supplied chart, so its chart-image error is zero. Applying `C-SLOW-CHART-NEAR-BOUNDARY-COUNT` with `rho_D=C D^(-2/3)` and `tau_D=0` bounds their number by

```text
O_{J,K}(D^(4/5) + D*rho_D) = O_{J,K}(D^(4/5)).
```

At most six distinct source points can be unit neighbors of one anchor: six is the sharp circular-packing maximum for unit-separated points on a unit circle. Multiplication by six proves the leaf-port count. This replaces the source report's weaker `O(D^(-1/2))` localization with the canonical `O(D^(-2/3))` theorem.

For the double-port class, `C-SLOW-CHART-DOUBLE-PORT-COUNT` directly gives `O_{J,K}(D^(4/5))` for actual diameter endpoints in any finite unit-separated sources, with two actual unit neighbors in one supplied chart. Its hypotheses allow arbitrary chart domains and retain every other source label. The union bound combines the two classes.

Finally, canonical `C-ZRF` and `C-WOODALL` give `q_j>=h_j>=ceil(D_j)`. Subtracting the covered population yields the stated uncovered lower bound. No chart extraction or source-level motion is used.

## Scope and regression checks

- The leaf count applies only to leaves whose sole contact has a positive coefficient in some normalized full-source KKT stress. It says nothing about zero-stress leaves.
- The double-port count concerns actual diameter endpoints and two actual neighbors in one chart. It does not infer an owner path, shared stress, or simultaneous move.
- Contact isolates remain included in `K_D`, in all unit-separation hypotheses, and in the full-source stress scope. They are never represented by chart coordinates unless separately supplied as actual chart images.
- The six-neighbor multiplicity is necessary; an anchor need not correspond to a unique leaf endpoint.
- Compared with `C-CII-SLOW-CHART-DOUBLE-PORT-COVER-EXCLUSION`, this adds the separately controlled positive-stress leaf class under the same slow-chart atlas. Compared with `C-FULLY-OCCUPIED-CHART-CONTACT-PORT-SCARCITY`, it removes full rectangular occupancy but requires pointwise `K/D` chart control.
