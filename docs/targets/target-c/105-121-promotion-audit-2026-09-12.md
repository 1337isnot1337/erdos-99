\[

\[
J(p)={1\over2}[\sin(2p)+\sqrt3(1-\cos(2p))-2p].

\[
\sin Q+\sqrt3(1-\cos Q)-Q={\sigma\over2}.

supports the conservative `sigma<=1.75e-3` contradiction and coefficient `0.9760812392179798...`.

The decoupled envelope near `0.00176783502507` is again diagnostic, not a universal barrier.
