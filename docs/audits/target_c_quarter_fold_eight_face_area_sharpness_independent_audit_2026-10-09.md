## Verdict

**PASS — promote `C-QUARTER-FOLD-EIGHT-FACE-AREA-INFIMUM-SHARP`.** Among finite unit-separated sources with a simple convex actual eight-sided unit-contact face admitting a nonconstant face-closed quarter-fold with a full unit-separated, triangle-free endpoint, the infimum of the face area is exactly `2 sqrt(3)`. Audit.223 gives the strict lower bound for every member; the family below approaches it. No member in the sequence is asserted to be globally minimizing.

## Independent construction

Fix `epsilon` with `0<epsilon<pi/12`, let `gamma=pi/3+epsilon`, and choose unit vectors `u,v` at angle `gamma`. At `alpha=0`, take the eight perimeter sites of the two-by-two parallelogram grid generated by `u,v`, omitting the center. Along each side, the boundary is two consecutive unit segments. Assign positive signs on the two opposite `u`-directed arcs and negative signs on the two opposite `v`-directed arcs. Under the canonical integration rule `U_{i+1}-U_i=s_iJq_i`, undo the common `J` rotation: the transformed side increments are `s_iq_i`, so the endpoint is the perimeter of the same two-by-two lattice grid with basis `u,-v`, again omitting its center. Opposite arcs cancel exactly, so the face closes.

For any lattice offset `ku+lv` with `|k|,|l|<=2`, the squared norm is

\[
k^2+l^2+2kl\cos\gamma.
\]

Among nonbasis offsets the minimum is `2-2cos(gamma)>1`; all offsets with one coefficient of magnitude two have squared norm at least `5-4cos(gamma)>3`, and offsets with both magnitudes two have squared norm at least `8-8cos(gamma)>4`. Thus every nonconsecutive old boundary pair is strictly longer than one. For the endpoint basis `u,-v`, the dot product is `-cos(gamma)`; the minimum among the nonbasis offsets `(1,1)` and `(1,-1)` is again `2-2cos(gamma)>1`, and offsets with a coefficient of magnitude two remain strictly longer than one by the same bounds. Therefore its full noncontact ledger is strict as well.

Now take `alpha>0` sufficiently small. Replace each straight two-edge side arc by an outward isosceles two-unit-edge arc with chord length `L=2cos(alpha)` and midpoint height `sin(alpha)`. Its unit edge directions are the chord direction rotated by `+alpha` and `-alpha`. The four corner positions are `0`, `Lu`, `L(u+v)`, and `Lv`. The boundary remains closed; the opposite `u` arcs cancel under their plus signs, and the opposite `v` arcs cancel under their minus signs. At the four old transition corners the exterior turns are `gamma-2alpha` and `pi-gamma-2alpha`; the two-edge arc midpoints turn by `2alpha`. For sufficiently small positive `alpha`, all turns are positive and the old eight-sided face is strictly convex.

For this fixed `epsilon`, the alpha-zero old and folded configurations have a finite noncontact ledger with positive margin above one. All constructed coordinates vary continuously with `alpha`, while the eight boundary edges remain exactly unit. Since there are finitely many pairs, for every sufficiently small positive `alpha` both the old source and the folded endpoint remain unit-separated with exactly the eight cycle contacts. In particular, the endpoint is triangle-free. The sign pattern is nonconstant and face-closed, so these are members of the audited eight-face class. No upper-diameter or optimality premise is part of that class.

The face area is the corner parallelogram plus four outward isosceles caps:

\[
\operatorname{Area}(F_{\epsilon,\alpha})
=4\cos^2\alpha\sin\gamma+4\cos\alpha\sin\alpha.
\]

For each fixed `epsilon`, choose `alpha` positive and small enough for the strict pair ledgers and for the cap-area term to tend to zero. Then let `epsilon` tend to zero. The areas tend to

\[
4\sin(\pi/3)=2\sqrt3.
\]

Together with Audit.223's strict lower bound, this proves the infimum is exactly `2 sqrt(3)` and that no positive uniform gap exists.

## Nonglobal scope and verifier boundary

Along this sequence, the old corner diagonal has squared length

\[
L^2|u+v|^2=4\cos^2\alpha(2+2\cos\gamma)\longrightarrow12.
\]

It is therefore greater than `7` for sufficiently small `epsilon,alpha`. A regular unit octagon has squared diameter `4+2sqrt(2)<7`, so those finite eight-point sources are strictly nonglobal. This is a scope check, not a global-minimizer counterexample.

No script encodes the family or its continuity margin. The offset calculation above is exact; continuity is used only after fixing `epsilon`, where finitely many strict noncontact inequalities have positive margin. The report's additional claims that the fold is nonaffine and that no old diameter changes are not needed for this infimum theorem and are not promoted here.
