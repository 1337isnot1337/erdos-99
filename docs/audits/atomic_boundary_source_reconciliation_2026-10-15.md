`(2pi/3-epsilon,pi)`, translated by `0`, `(10L,0)` and `(20L,0)`.
For each mesh, `0<cos(pi/3+epsilon)<1/2`. A nonzero difference has square
`r^2+s^2+2rs cos(pi/3+epsilon)`; only the two signed basis steps have
length one. Opposite signs give
`(|r|-|s|)^2+2|rs|(1-cos)>1` outside those steps; equal signs are also
strict except the steps. Parity makes each contact graph bipartite.
