## Evidence labels used here

- **Author-checked claim:** the authors report checking the argument or computation; no independent review is implied.
- **Unreviewed proposal:** a question or approach awaiting mathematical review.
- **Conditional claim:** a result whose stated conclusion depends on an unresolved hypothesis.
- **Reproduced finite evidence:** a computation or certificate rerun for exactly its finite scope.
- **Independently agent-audited:** an agent independent of the authors checked a stated scope; this is not human review.
- **Human-reviewed mathematical result:** a human independent of the authors checked the proof and its stated scope.
- **Rejected or superseded:** a claim found invalid or replaced by a better-scoped result; retain the reason and link to the disposition.
- **Canonical accepted theorem:** a result explicitly promoted by the maintainers after independent review. Merge order alone does not confer this status.

The three task questions are open proposals. The supplied lattice baseline is reproduced finite evidence only for its finite lattice model. Neither the tasks nor their labels imply a result about all planar configurations.
