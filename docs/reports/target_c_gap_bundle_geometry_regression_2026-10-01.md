| Name | Coordinates | Layer |

| l+ | (-10,30) | 1 |
| l- | (-10,-30) | 1 |

Every pair distance is strictly greater than one and at most 65. Exactly four
pairs have distance 65: `vx+`, `vx-`, `l+r-`, and `l-r+`. The hull order is
`v,l-,r-,x-,x+,r+,l+`, with every listed point extreme. The two independent matching
diameters cross each other and both V spokes; all other pairs of diameter edges
share v or cross. The actual diameter graph is a V and two matching components,
hence a forest.

Two vertical proper crosscuts, at `x=-20` and `x=0`, realize the listed layers.
These are topological crosscuts, **not actual contact trains**.

With all four diameter weights equal to one, the interval rows are

```
 ( 16, 16), (-16,-16), (0,-60), (0,60).
```

They have rank two and positive weighted sum zero. Their gap edges are two copies
of `{0,2}` and two copies of `{1,2}`. The full gap graph is connected and

These weights certify cut identities. The source has no unit contacts, so they

## 3. Arbitrarily many layers and arbitrarily round hulls

Fix an integer `J>=1` and rational `0<t<=1/10`. Put `m=J+1`, and take distinct
rational parameters `5t<z_1<...<z_J<1`. Define

\[
v=(-1,0),\qquad
X_\pm=\left({1-3t^2\over1+t^2},\ \pm{4t\over1+t^2}\right),
\]
\[
c_j={1-z_j^2\over1+z_j^2},\quad
s_j={2z_j\over1+z_j^2},\quad
L_j^\pm=(-c_j,\pm s_j),\quad R_j^\pm=(c_j,\pm s_j).
\]

Assign v to layer 0, both `L_j` points to layer j, and every R and X point to
layer m. Since `-c_j` increases strictly, vertical crosscuts realize these layers.

### Exact diameter and extremality

All L and R points lie on the unit circle. Their only diameter pairs are the
antipodal pairs `L_j+ R_j-` and `L_j- R_j+`. Moreover `|vX_+|=|vX_-|=2`.

The only potentially excessive distances are between X and a circle point. For
`Q=(x,y)` on the circle, `|X_+Q|<=2` is equivalent to

\[
(1-3t^2)(1+x)\ge4ty.
\]

For either left circle point the stronger absolute-value inequality reduces to
`z_j(1-3t^2)>4t`, which holds for `z_j>5t` and `t<=1/10`. For a right point,
`1+x>=1` and `|y|<=1`, so `1-3t^2>4t` suffices. Thus all X-circle distances are
strictly below two. Distances from v to circle points are also strict, and
`|X_+X_-|=8t/(1+t^2)<2`.

Consequently the actual diameter graph has exactly `2m` edges: the common V and
`J` two-edge antipodal matchings. Every source point is a diameter endpoint and
is extreme. Directly, if `ab` is a diameter vector r, then for any other source z,

\[
(z-a)\cdot r\ge |z-a|^2/2>0,
\quad (z-b)\cdot r\le-|z-b|^2/2<0.
\]

### Full rank, circulation, reactions, common witness cell

The gap graph consists of two edges `{j,m}` for each `j=0,...,m-1`. Its simple
support is a star. Its rows are opposite multiples of the suffix indicators
`1_[j+1,m]`, a basis of `R^m`. Therefore its rank is m, it is connected and
bridgeless, and equal positive weights give transverse circulation in every bundle.

The V directions have angles `+/-2 arctan(t)`, while matching diameter directions
have angles `+/-2 arctan(z_j)`. The V spokes are therefore consecutive global
diameter directions bracketing u and cross all selected cuts.

The axial masses are

\[
A_i={4(1-t^2)\over1+t^2}+4\sum_{j<i}c_j>0.
\]

For each internal layer j, `A_j-A_(j+1)=-4c_j` is nonzero and is generated by
its two actual matching diameter bars with opposite transverse signs.

To obtain a unit-separated contact-free source, multiply every coordinate by twice
the least common multiple of all coordinate denominators. All coordinates then
become even integers; distinct points are at least two apart. This preserves every
diameter incidence, layer, rank, flux sign, and normalized hull shape.

### Roundness, with a stated order of limits

Choose

\[
t={1\over100J},\qquad
z_j=5t+(1-5t){j\over J+1}.
\]

The L/R circle vertices have maximum angular gap at most
`gamma_J=20t+4/(J+1)=O(1/J)`. This follows from `theta_j=2 arctan(z_j)`, the
bound `|arctan a-arctan b|<=|a-b|`, and the four reflected copies. Their convex
hull contains the disk of radius `cos(gamma_J/2)` for large J. All points lie in
the disk of radius `sqrt(1+8t^2/(1+t^2))`. Thus the normalized hull converges in
Hausdorff distance to the unit disk, while m tends to infinity. The normalized
diameter is exactly two throughout.

This is a geometric regression with unbounded cardinality and roundness, not an
unbounded family of global minimizers. Integer rescaling does not establish a
quadratic near-optimal packing population or active unit contacts.
