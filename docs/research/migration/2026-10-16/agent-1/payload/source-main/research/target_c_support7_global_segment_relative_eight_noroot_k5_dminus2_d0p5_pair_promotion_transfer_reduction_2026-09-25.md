## Pair-to-complete transfer

Let `b` be a touched pair block and promote it to complete by adding its missing third root row. Let `H` be the original touched complete-root span, let `h_b` be the complete-root row at `b`, and put `H'=H+<h_b>`. Since the original complete rows are independent, `dim(H'/H)<=1`.

Pair and complete states impose no coefficient constraint at `b`, so promotion leaves `P`, its five-dimensional nullspace, the singleton basis, and generalized weights unchanged.

The exact verifier normal form has local quotient vectors
`(1,0),(0,1),(1,-1)`. For any `alpha in null(P)`, the pair and promoted local corrections cancel the same quotient demand. Their difference lies in the kernel of the local map `Q^3->Q^2`, generated by `(1,-1,-1)`. In the 15-dimensional relation coordinate this is exactly a multiple of the complete-root row
`h_b=M_0-M_1-M_2`.

Therefore, if `E_pair` and `E_prom` are the external relation spans,
`H'+E_pair=H'+E_prom`. Writing
`r_pair=dim((H+E_pair)/H)` and
`r_prom=dim((H'+E_prom)/H')`, we get
`r_pair<=r_prom+1`.

This is exact linear algebra in the committed coordinate model.

## Why the promoted state is covered by the `p=3` certificates

For `m=3`, promotion adds one row to an independent complete-root set, so promoted touched-complete nullity is at most one, exactly the range scanned by the current-main three-support certificate.
