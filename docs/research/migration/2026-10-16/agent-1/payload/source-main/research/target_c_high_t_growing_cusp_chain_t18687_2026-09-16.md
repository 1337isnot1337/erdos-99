## 1. Fixed `r` gives an eventual theorem

For each positive integer `r`, the fixed-chain theorem states that there is a threshold `D_0(r)` such that every high-`t` survivor with

\[
D\ge D_0(r)
\]

contains an `r`-strip chain satisfying its full finite list of conclusions. Increase `D_0(r)` if necessary so that the following also hold whenever the theorem is invoked:

1. the row/column asymptotic errors in all stated lower bounds are smaller than `1/r` times their leading terms;
2. the maximum common-ladder crossing excess differs from the explicit fixed-`r` upper bound by less than `1/r`;
3. the block-translation theorem applies to every one of the finitely many contiguous subblocks;
4. `D_0(r)>=r^2`.

Replace `D_0(r)` by the monotone envelope

\[
\widehat D_0(r):=\max_{1\le s\le r}D_0(s).
\tag{GC.8}
\]

Then `\widehat D_0(r)` is nondecreasing and still finite for every fixed `r`.

## 2. Choose an increasing diagonal depth

Along the hypothetical survivor sequence define

\[
r_D
:=\max\{r\ge1:\widehat D_0(r)\le D\}.
\tag{GC.9}
\]

If the set is temporarily empty, ignore those finitely many initial terms. Because every fixed `\widehat D_0(r)` is finite and `D->infinity`,

\[
r_D\to\infty.
\tag{GC.10}
\]

Condition 4 in the definition of the thresholds gives

\[
D\ge\widehat D_0(r_D)\ge r_D^2,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{r_D\le D^{1/2}.}
\]

By construction, the complete fixed-chain theorem and every finite subblock theorem are valid at depth `r_D` on the selected source.

This is a standard diagonal argument; it makes no uniform-in-`r` assertion about the original `o(\cdot)` terms.

## 3. The entire switching support becomes sublinear

The fixed-chain support bound is

\[
\sum_{i<r_D}|H_{sw,i}|
<1{,}109{,}000r_D.
\tag{GC.11}
\]

Since `r_D<=D^{1/2}`,

\[
{1\over D}
\sum_{i<r_D}|H_{sw,i}|
< {1{,}109{,}000\over D^{1/2}}
\longrightarrow0.
\]

This proves (GC.2).

Because the chain is wholly on one side of the common routing hub, different interfaces have disjoint switching sets. Reaction nondegeneracy makes every switching set nonempty. Therefore

\[
r_D
\le\left|\bigsqcup_{i<r_D}H_{sw,i}\right|
<1{,}109{,}000r_D,
\]

which proves (GC.4).

For each interface choose a minimal one/two-bar exact reaction generator `J_i`. The sets `J_i` are pairwise disjoint and satisfy

\[
1\le|J_i|\le2.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
r_D\le\left|\bigsqcup_{i<r_D}J_i\right|\le2r_D.}
\tag{GC.12}
\]

The high-`t` branch therefore requires an unbounded number of distinct local diameter blockers along the growing chain, but only sublinearly many relative to `D`.

The fixed-chain KKT-mass bound gives

<{1{,}109{,}000r_D\over D}.

Using `r_D<=D^{1/2}`,

\[
\sum_{i<r_D}B_i

This proves (GC.3).

Every selected reaction generator lies inside its interface switching set, so the sum of the global normalized diameter multipliers of **all** selected reaction-generating bars is also `o(1)`.

This is a genuine asymptotic concentration statement: an unbounded number of distinct exact local reactions is carried by a set whose total global diameter KKT mass vanishes.

For the common retained columns, the fixed-chain Markov estimate has leading scale

The threshold choice absorbs its fixed-`r` remainder into a sequence tending to zero, and `r_D/D<=D^{-1/2}`. Hence

## 8. Scope audit

- The theorem passes to a subsequence and chooses `r_D` by diagonalization. It does not give an explicit universal formula for `r_D` beyond the harmless upper choice `D^(1/2)`.
- The original fixed-`r` asymptotic estimates are not assumed uniform in `r`; the threshold construction is exactly what avoids that mistake.
- `o(1)` total switching KKT mass does not imply an individual multiplier lower or upper bound beyond positivity.
- The number of switching bars still tends to infinity; only its ratio to `D` tends to zero.
- TB2 endpoint clusters are `o(D)`, not necessarily `O(1)`, in the growing-depth diagonal.
- No C-II or Level-I closure is claimed.

**Disposition:** upgraded the fixed finite-cusp normal form to a growing-depth ordered cusp chain with sublinear moving diameter support and vanishing total switching KKT mass. C-II and Level I remain open.
