### Theorem AF.1 — exact force-carrying antipodal pair

For every exact-antipodal interface on a one-sided high-`t` shore there are two **distinct actual switching diameter bars**

\[

with consistently oriented unit diameter directions `d_i^-,d_i^+` and positive coefficients `lambda_i^-,lambda_i^+>0` such that

\[
\boxed{

=D\bigl(\lambda_i^-d_i^-+\lambda_i^+d_i^+\bigr),
}
\tag{AF.2}
\]

while the two directions lie on opposite sides of the exact reaction axis:

\[
\boxed{
(d_i^-\cdot Je_i)(d_i^+\cdot Je_i)<0,
\qquad e_i:={G_i\over|G_i|}=\pm u_i.
}
\tag{AF.3}
\]

Equivalently, `e_i` lies in the **strict interior** of the positive cone generated by the two selected switching diameter directions.  Thus the two-bar exact KKT compression can always be chosen to be an antipodal blocker pair, not merely an unrelated Caratheodory pair.

If their lifted angular deviations from `e_i` are `p_i,q_i>0`, with `p_i+q_i<pi`, then the coefficients may be chosen explicitly as

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_i^-={|G_i|\over D}{\sin q_i\over\sin(p_i+q_i)},
\qquad
\lambda_i^+={|G_i|\over D}{\sin p_i\over\sin(p_i+q_i)}.
}
\tag{AF.4}

No lower or upper bound on these compressed coefficients is claimed; the denominator may be small.
