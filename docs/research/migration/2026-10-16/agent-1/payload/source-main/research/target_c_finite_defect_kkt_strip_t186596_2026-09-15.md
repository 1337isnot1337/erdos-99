## 6. Finite transverse-network defect

Let `Gamma(tau)` be the set of complete stopped trains crossing `tau` in a strict
rhombus.  If a train crosses `tau_i` but not `tau_{i+1}`, its midpoint path enters
`R_i` at its unique crossing with `tau_i`.  Two complete trains meet in at most one
strict rhombus, so it cannot leave through `tau_i` again; by assumption it does not
leave through `tau_{i+1}`.  It must terminate in `R_i` and therefore consumes one of
the `L_i` terminal occurrences.  The reverse difference is identical.  Thus

\[
\boxed{
|\Gamma(\tau_i)\triangle\Gamma(\tau_{i+1})|
\le L_i\le845674.
}
\tag{FD596.20}
\]

Apart from this fixed-size defect set, every transverse train crossing one boundary
also crosses the other.  The strip is therefore a through-going rhombic train band
with only finitely many terminal/network defects.
