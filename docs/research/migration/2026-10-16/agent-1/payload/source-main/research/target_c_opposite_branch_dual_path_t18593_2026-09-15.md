## 3. Optimized same-branch exclusion

\[
e_\tau\ge(1-\cos\alpha)+(1-\cos\beta)-o(1).
\tag{OB.10}
\]

Assume for contradiction that the two terminals use the same oriented normal branch.  Their circular normal separation is then at most

\[
\alpha+\beta+o(1).
\]

By convexity,

\[
2\left(1-\cos{\alpha+\beta\over2}\right)

\[
