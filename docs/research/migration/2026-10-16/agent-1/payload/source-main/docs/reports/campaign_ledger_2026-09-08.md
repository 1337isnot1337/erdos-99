## Closing theorem package and logical limit

The shortest existing route-level sufficient package remains **C-I and C-II
together**, with the interior-isolate alternatives retained in C-II. Neither
the new Target A inequality nor the repaired C-II accounting closes that package.
Calling the package a single new theorem would not reduce its mathematical content.

A single exact globally sufficient formulation is the following uniform finite
release-or-descent statement:

> There is `n0` such that every contact-minimal, diametrally saturated,
> triangle-free global minimizer on `n>=n0` points admits a unit-separated
> `n`-point competitor of diameter at most `D(n)` whose contact graph is a
> proper subgraph of the original, or admits a competitor of diameter strictly
> below `D(n)`.

The first outcome contradicts contact minimality (the subgraph remains
triangle-free); the second contradicts global minimum diameter. Canonical
normalization would then close all original quantifiers. This statement is
unproved, not advertised as a new reduction beyond the route gates.
