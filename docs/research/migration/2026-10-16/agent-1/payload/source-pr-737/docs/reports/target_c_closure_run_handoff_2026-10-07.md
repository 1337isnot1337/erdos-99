fixed `M>=3` and sufficiently small positive curvature depending on M.

corner and omitting a target-grid center. If the old wall contains

inside the old source hull; no new contacts are created and two old wall
contacts disappear. Oler bounds the full old population by N, leaving
`z=N-1,N`.

Checkpoint 15 claims the following independently reviewable chain:

   statement force the complete limiting triangular chart.

   rate vector. The projected polytope is the convex hull of permutations
   of the prescribed wall angular rates.
3. The radial stress demand forces an equality permutation. The sector
   recurrences then determine every moving velocity.
4. Exact sorting packets realize these velocities and contain unit
   triangles. They refute a proposed strict energy gap for every M.
5. A relative-interior normal-cone argument constructs positive pinned
   stresses on these auxiliary packets. Their limiting contact matrices
   have full moving-coordinate rank, yielding local uniqueness radius
   proportional to curvature.
6. Exhaustive first-velocity convergence puts every competing placement
   within that radius. It must be an exact sorting packet and contain a
   unit triangle.

The finite sorting geometry, energy equalities and modular ranks are
certified. The all-M normal-cone continuation, compactness classification
and source-dependent onset are analytic candidate claims requiring audit.

an explicitly assumed central-vacancy limiting chart; it does not derive

wall class. An arbitrary canonical source has not been shown to contain
