## Complete proof reconstruction

### 1. Geometry, collar, and incidence depth

Use lattice coordinates with `q(a,b)=a^2+ab+b^2` and phase
`v=(1/5,1/7)`. The source is
`X={(i,j):i,j>=0,i+j<=M}`. The three strict neighbors of a source label
are row labels `x`, `x-(1,0)`, and `x-(0,1)`. Their squared distances are
`109/1225`, `669/1225`, and `739/1225`. Checking the other unit lattice
directions excludes them. All other nonzero integer differences have
`q>=3`, since this form is zero or one modulo three, and `|v|<1/3` keeps
them farther than one.

The complete collar consists of
`Z={(i,j):i,j>=0,i+j<=M-1}` and the three disjoint exterior strips
`(-1,j)`, `(i,-1)`, `(i,M-i)`, with each range from zero through `M`.
The half-plane distance bounds exclude labels with a coordinate at most
`-2` or sum at least `M+1`. The remaining exceptional corner labels have
squared distances `2524/1225`, `1264/1225`, and `1404/1225`, all above one.
Every listed row is a strict source neighbor. Thus no exterior sinks have
been omitted, and `|O|=3M+3`.

Most importantly, Section 102 defines depth by **incidence**:

`X^circ={x:N(x) subseteq Z}={(i,j):i,j>=1,i+j<=M-1}`.

The identity row matching makes the entire deep restriction independent.
Its only column basis is therefore the whole deep set. A Section-102 basis
must contain every one of these columns. Geometric distance greater than
one from the hull is sufficient for depth but is not its definition.

### 2. The complete fan tube and basis closure

The fan consists of the triangle `(1,1),(2,1),(1,2)`, the two short spokes
to `(0,1)` and `(2,0)`, and the vertical chain ending at `(1,M-1)`.
It has `M+2` vertices and unit edges and gives the four stated geometric
sectors. Exact projection gives the local tube tables in the README.
Their exhaustion outside the finite box is justified by
`q(w)>=3 max(|w_1|,|w_2|)^2/4`; no numerical truncation hypothesis is used.

Taking their translated union gives exactly

`T_Z={(0,j):0<=j<=M-1} union {(1,j):0<=j<=M-2} union {(2,0),(2,1)}`

and `T_O={(-1,1),(2,-1),(1,M-1)}`. Hence `|T_Z|=2M+1` and `|T_O|=3`.

Every matched row of a basis source on the fan belongs to the tube, since
it is a strict response neighbor of that fan point. Thus removing all fan
sources, all tube rows, and all matched source partners of `T_Z` is exactly
the Section-174 basis closure. Surviving basis partners and interior rows
remain in the same geometric sector. In the two displayed matchings all
surviving basis and interior row counts are `(0,g,0,0)`, where
`g=(M^2-3M-2)/2`.

### 3. Witness matchings and admissibility

The predecessor's two row-shift chains are disjoint, use strict conflict
edges, and terminate at distinct top-side sources. It contains every deep
source and omits all three boundary fan endpoints. It is admissible.

The submitted relaxed matching also has disjoint image ranges and is
`Z`-perfect. Its complement is the fan except `(1,1)`, so all `M+1`
nonbasis sources are on the fan. However, it omits
`{(2,1)} union {(1,j):2<=j<=M-2}`, exactly `M-2` incidence-deep columns.
At `M=5` the omitted set is `(1,2),(1,3),(2,1)`, while the historical
geometric-distance predicate has an empty deep set. This is a concrete
counterexample to that old admissibility test.

I independently found another relaxed witness before reading the submitted
matching. It fixes `(0,0),(1,0)`, maps `(0,j)` to `(0,j+1)` for `j>=1`,
maps `(1,j)` to `(2,j)` for `j>=1`, and maps `(i,j)` to `(i+1,j)` for
`i>=2`. It has the same relaxed optimum but a different omitted deep set.
This is an additional check of the class distinction, not a needed new
component of the submitted proof.

### 4. All-basis optimum

For an arbitrary `Z`-perfect matching, retain **all** nonbasis sources in
the sector terminal counts for the relaxed comparison. The exterior
partition and `a-max(a-b,0)=min(a,b)` give

`Xi-|T_Z|=|O|-|T_O|-|T_Z|-sum_i min(|C_i|,|O_i|)<=M-1`.

The relaxed witness has no surviving nonbasis source in any sector and
attains `M-1`. The full-Schur linkage proof uses all nonbasis sources, so
the tube interception and sector cut apply without falsely discarding
omitted deep columns.

Now impose Section 102. All `M+1` nonbasis sources are boundary terminals.
Only three boundary sources lie on the fan. Let `t` be the number of these
three that are nonbasis. Each exterior row is a leaf with one source
neighbor. The three exterior tube rows attach precisely to the boundary
fan endpoints. Every surviving boundary terminal therefore has a distinct
exterior sink outside the tube in its sector. Its sink survives because
its source is a nonbasis column and is not removed as a matching partner.
Thus `|O_i|>=|C_i|`, and closure removes a nonbasis source only on the fan.
Consequently

`sum_i min(|C_i|,|O_i|)=M+1-t`, so `Xi-|T_Z|=t-2<=1`.

The predecessor has `t=3` and attains equality. This proves the optimum for
**every** admissible basis and its matching for every `M>=5`; the finite
search is supplementary.

The explicit predecessor ledger is `C_i=(0,M-3,1,0)`,
`O_i=(0,2M-4,2,0)`, and `|O_iso|=M+2`. The relaxed ledger is
`C_i=0`, `O_i=(0,2M-5,0,0)`, and `|O_iso|=M+5`. Directly applying the
unique exterior source-neighbor rule verifies all endpoints of those ranges
and their complete disjoint partition of `O`.

### 5. Full versus boundary Schur response

The injective diagonal conflict matching saturates the entire source, so
the full generic incidence matrix has rank `|X|`. For either displayed
`Z`-perfect matching, block elimination gives full Schur rank
`|X|-|Z|=M+1` and actual right deficiency `|O|-(M+1)=2M+2`.
The symmetric-difference linkage argument is valid and gives the same
rank. This concerns independent edge indeterminates, not an all-ones
specialization.

For the predecessor, the full complement is exactly `C^partial`, so the
canonical boundary formula applies. For the relaxed witness, the
boundary-only block has just three columns and rank three; its omitted
deep block has rank `M-2`, and those columns are **not loops**. Using
`|O|-rank(S_boundary)` there would give `3M`, while actual `h` is `2M+2`.
The corrected package retains the full complement and labels the canonical
interface false. This resolves the load-bearing error.

The admissible cut's loss relative to the actual deficiency is
`(2M+2)-1=2M+1`; the relaxed full-Schur cut loses `M+3`.
