## PR89: complete proof/scope reconstruction

The statement concerns every integer m>=2, but only three explicitly supplied
translations of one hexagonal lattice response fixture. It does not concern every
translation, source, basis optimum, minimizer, cardinality in the Erdős question,
or joint phase-averaged population quantity. There is no genuine-source j_*.
The source contains unit triangles and no contact-isolated labels; all
3m^2+3m+1 labels are retained in its response calculation.

1. Four radial offsets and the complete collar.
For v=(a,b), the three inequalities Q(v)<2a+b, Q(v)<a+2b and Q(v)<b-a make
the offsets (-1,0),(0,-1),(1,-1) strict conflicts, together with (0,0).
The opposite unit directions have squared length greater than one. Any further
nonzero triangular-lattice vector has norm at least sqrt(3), and |v|<1/10
excludes it by the reverse triangle inequality. Thus the local adjacency is
exhaustive and has no hidden zero/unit equality.

The complete open hull collar includes X_m, the 6m noncorner sites on shell
m+1, and exactly the three stated negative-facing shell corners. Noncorner
shell points are sqrt(3)/2 from the old hull and remain strictly within one
under these translations. At a shell corner the closest old corner stays the
projection because the adjacent outward products remain positive; the same
unit-direction test selects the three corners. Points beyond that shell violate
an axial hull inequality by at least two, so their distance remains above one.
Hence |Y_in|=3m^2+9m+4; neighborless collar rows are included.

2. Interior rows and actual Schur rank.
The interior integer indices satisfy -m<=i,j,i+j<=m-1. They give |Z|=3m^2;
the complement has |C|=3m+1. Matching each interior row to its own unshifted
source is Section-102 admissible: any source not in this basis has its own
exterior neighbor, so every incidence-deep column is included. All source
columns are independent because the own-row square submatrix is triangular
under i+2j, with every other supported row strictly lower in that functional.
The interior block has the same property. Thus d_int=0, C=C^partial, and the
bordered determinant gives rank(S)=3m+1. This rank computation is reconstructed
directly, not inferred from local locking, density or global optimality.

3. Full tube, including the m=2 boundary case.
The fan is exactly the union of three complete segments: the length-2m horizontal
segment, the length-m diagonal, and the short third side of the unit triangle.
It has 3m+1 unit edges/vertices. The short segment adds no tube rows. The local
unit-edge tables are exact finite certificates with an explicit exhaustive
coordinate bound. Their horizontal union has 6m+4 indices; the diagonal union
has 3m+4. The seven listed intersections are present already at m=2, so the
full tube has 9m+1 rows. For m>=3, horizontal/diagonal exterior counts are 6/3
with interior intersection. At m=2, the diagonal count rises to five, while
two intersecting rows are exterior, leaving the same total nine. Therefore
|T_O|=9 and |T_Z|=9m-8 for every declared m.

4. Exact sectors and basis closure.
The sectors can independently be represented by the following convex polygons
in axial coordinates, avoiding any response-component identification:
triangle [(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)]; upper left
[(-m,0),(0,0),(0,1),(1-m,m),(-m,m)]; upper right
[(1,0),(m,0),(0,m),(1-m,m)]; bottom
[(-m,0),(0,-m),(m,-m),(m,0)]. Closing the own-row basis deletes exactly the
fan source vertices and own source indices of T_Z. Exterior own rows do not
authorize deleting their terminal source: that distinction is important at m=2.

The displayed basis intervals yield, respectively,
0, (m-1)(m-2)/2, (m-2)(m-3), (3m^2-5m+2)/2, with the m=2/3 empty cases
handled correctly. Applying all four offsets to every surviving source yields
