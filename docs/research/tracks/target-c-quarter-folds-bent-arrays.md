## What the route establishes conditionally

The seven reports refine two different endpoint families. The quarter-fold work assumes a full-source, same-cardinality, unit-separated placement with the exact old contact graph and diameter at most the source diameter. It restricts what such an endpoint could look like. The array work assumes an actual complete bent rhombic array in a canonical source and rules out large whole-core instances through diameter topology and boundary loading. Neither family constructs a finite competitor from an arbitrary surviving C-II core.

## Reproduced finite checks

The seven destination verifiers completed with exit status zero on the frozen source-identical scripts. Their rational and algebraic checks cover the declared finite fixtures: arc-count vectors and bowed cycles; face degrees 5–32 and local isolate/ray examples; pinned 16-site grids and all local arc-star sign choices; the seven surviving large-face packing cases; 22 defect classes and integer-circle counts through 4096; 81 complete-array fixtures with all pair bounds; and the boundary-attachment lens, star, determinant, and pair checks.

These executions reproduce finite arithmetic and regression examples. They do not prove the analytic compactness, universal rank, full-source global optimality, endpoint existence, complete-array extraction, or an asymptotic conclusion by enumeration. The destination theorem-state remains the authority; C-II and Level I remain open.

## Exact next obligations

1. Extract an actual complete bent array with both dimensions proportional to D from every remaining canonical survivor, with all actual contact and diameter constraints retained; or prove a different source-level endpoint in the complementary regime.
2. For the quarter-fold branch, construct a nonaffine full-source endpoint satisfying every lower and upper pair constraint, including isolates and inactive upper pairs, with the face-sign restrictions above; or derive a finite competitor without assuming that endpoint exists.
3. For complete arrays, reconcile the quadratic isolate burden with linear external boundary attachment and retain the exact whole-core hypotheses. A supplied finite array, isolated-core transform, or population inequality alone is not a C-II contradiction.
