# Research archive intake and scientific acceptance

A merge can preserve a useful artifact without accepting its mathematics.
[Review policy](../REVIEW_POLICY.md) controls claims; [STATE](../STATE.md) and the
manifest alone control canonical lifecycle. The [exact-head ledger](pr-ledger.json)
records scientific scope separately from GitHub publication. Initial records are
empty; no historical PR has been retrospectively certified by this workflow.

| Lane | Acceptance obligation |
| --- | --- |
| Archive | Authenticate source identity/modes, full inventory, provenance, syntax and available pins for isolated new research paths. Preserve explicit scientific and execution obligations. |
| Validated research | Reconstruct the stated proof/mechanism and complete applicable source-bound checks within a declared scope. |
| Shared tooling | Review changed behavior and affected consumers; run focused checks and required full verification. |
| Canonical promotion | Complete applicable full verification, one independent AI full-proof/bridge audit on the exact SHA, and the explicit theorem-state transaction. |

Archive intake may preserve new isolated research under `research/` or
`research-packages/` while its scientific review remains `partially_reviewed`.
It must not change existing modules, shared code, automatic entrypoints, workflows,
canonical certificates or their inputs. Known correctness objections, misleading
scope, ambiguous provenance and source integrity failures require resolution;
they do not enter through the archive lane. Preserve frozen predecessors.

Source-only review is not a replay, a proof audit or an endorsed premise. Record
the complete changed-file inventory and exact source SHA/tree, checks actually
performed, inherited author evidence, unresolved dependencies, attribution and
one next action. A timeout is an outstanding execution obligation, not a negative
mathematical result. A preserved claim may be wrong; independently review it before
relying on it. Useful explicit regressions belong in their accurately scoped lane.

An archival label does not override the localized planner. A reviewer must resolve
its impact against actual declarations and reachability; an unresolved
`full-verification-required` result still requires the merging agent's replay.
This policy does not introduce a permissive planner exemption or bypass declared
mathematical inputs. Existing isolated replay remains optional and owner-gated.
Publication, deferred replay and mathematical approval are recorded distinctly.

Use [delta intake](pr-intake.md) to avoid reviewing unchanged heads repeatedly.
The ledger/cache never merge code, close a PR or promote a theorem. A head or
relevant dependency change invalidates readiness and requires reviewing its delta.
