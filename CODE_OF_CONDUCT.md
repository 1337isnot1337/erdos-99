# Community conduct

Erdős Problem 99 is open to people with different backgrounds, disciplines and research tools. Treat participants with patience and respect. Discuss mathematical claims, evidence and reasoning; do not attack or stereotype contributors.

Keep criticism specific, proportionate and useful. Credit prior work, disclose relevant conflicts, and accept corrections without harassment. Do not post private information, confidential material, threats, or discriminatory or sexualized abuse.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict accounts when needed to protect participants. To report a concern, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub. This code of conduct applies across Issues, Discussions, pull requests and project communication spaces.
